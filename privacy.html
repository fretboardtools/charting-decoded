What this covers

This site itself does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and does not operate its own checkout.

When you buy a product, payment and checkout are handled entirely by our payment processor (currently Ko-fi and/or Creem). They collect what's needed to process payment and deliver your files — typically your email address and payment details — and share only the minimum with us needed to fulfill the order and provide support (your email address and order details).

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

We don't control what these third parties do with data beyond what they disclose in their own policies.
